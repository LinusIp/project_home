A-001

Design concept

Minimalist high-tech

In a Central Asian hovli, rooms face inward onto a private yard and the street sees only a wall. This villa keeps that plan and rebuilds it in concrete, black metal and glass.

A 3 m monolithic wall encloses the whole plot. Inside it, a U-shaped house wraps three sides of a 10 × 20 m pool. Every family room opens onto the water through glass that slides away completely. From the street you see one steel gate and a glowing slot of lobby glass.

The ground floor is a heavy concrete base. The first floor is a lighter matte-black box that sits on it and cantilevers 4 m past it at the master bedroom. All plant, parking and staff space goes into the basement, so the two floors above hold nothing but living space.

Monolith

Pure rectangular volumes with flat roofs. Hidden parapets and concealed gutters leave no visible edge detail.

Void

The pool is the centre of the plan. The 7 m lobby void is the one vertical moment, and the stair floats through it.

Transparency

Frameless floor-to-ceiling glass runs 124 m along the courtyard faces. Walls pocket away and the floor continues to the pool edge at the same level.

Concealment

Nothing technical shows. Lighting is linear LED in reveals, doors and joinery are handleless, and speakers, sensors and blinds sit flush.

Technical systems

Mechanical · Electrical · Plumbing · Controls
01 · Engineering room B.05

How one basement room serves all three floors

Cooling. A water-cooled chiller makes chilled water at 6/12 °C, and dry coolers on the roof deck reject its heat. Pumps push the water up the riser in the concrete core to fan-coil units hidden in ceiling bulkheads, one per room, each with its own thermostat. The air-handling unit supplies filtered fresh air through a heat-recovery wheel to every room.

Water. Mains water fills two 15 m³ tanks, which hold one day of supply plus a firefighting reserve. It passes a UV steriliser, and a booster set holds 4 bar at every tap. Heat-pump cylinders on each floor provide hot water.

Power. The utility feed enters the main LV switchboard. If the grid fails, an automatic transfer switch starts the 250 kVA generator within 10 seconds. Sub-boards on each floor run off the riser, and roof PV offsets daytime load.

02 · Server room B.11

Cooling, UPS and fire safety

The network, security recording and AV sources all live here, so the room is built to keep running when everything else fails.

CoolingIn-row precision units, N+1, holding 22 °C ± 1 and 45 % RH. The hot aisle is contained, and the cooling is independent of the house chilled water.
PowerA 10 kVA online double-conversion UPS gives about 30 minutes at full load, long enough for the generator to take over.
FireAspirating smoke detection triggers a clean-agent gas release. No water is used, walls are 2-hour rated and doors seal automatically.
CablingOverhead ladder tray keeps copper and fibre apart. Fibre and Cat 6A run to a rack on each floor, and all cameras and sensors are PoE.
MonitoringLeak-detection tape, door access control and temperature alarms all report to the building management system and your phone.
Adjacent storageRoom B.12 holds spares and shares the server room's controlled atmosphere.
03 · Pool plant B.10

Filtration and the hidden plant room

The water surface sits flush with the deck. It spills over all four edges into a slotted stone grate and drains to a 12 m³ balance tank under the east deck. Variable-speed pumps draw from the tank through glass-media filters, a UV steriliser, a heat pump and a salt chlorinator. Clean water returns through jets in the pool floor, so nothing shows on the walls.

Probes track chlorine, pH and temperature continuously and dose automatically. The plant is reached from the service corridor or through a flush stainless hatch in the deck. From the courtyard there is nothing to see.

04 · Smart home

One system for the whole house

Every system runs on one KNX/IP backbone with its server in B.11. Flush touch panels on each floor, a phone app and presence sensors all control it, and each scene touches lighting, climate, blinds and audio together.

05 · Structure + envelope

Frame, insulation and smart glass

FrameReinforced concrete columns and cores with 300 mm post-tensioned flat slabs. Spans reach 10 m with no downstand beams, so ceilings stay flat for the LED reveals.
CantileverThe master suite projects 4 m on post-tensioned upstand beams hidden inside the first-floor walls, and the terrace uses thermally broken steel.
BasementWatertight concrete box inside a secant-piled perimeter. The pool has its own concrete shell on a separate raft, isolated by a movement joint.
RoofInverted roof with 150 mm insulation and concealed gutters behind a 600 mm parapet. It doubles as the plant and PV deck.
Walls100 mm mineral wool behind ventilated concrete and black metal rainscreens. Slab edges are thermally broken.
GlazingDouble low-iron solar-control glass (Ug 1.0, g 0.28) in minimal flush tracks that pocket fully into the walls.
