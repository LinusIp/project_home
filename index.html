A-001 · Design concept

A Courtyard House, Rebuilt in Concrete and Glass

In a Central Asian hovli, rooms face inward onto a private yard and the street sees only a wall. This villa keeps that plan and rebuilds it in concrete, black metal and glass.

A 3 m monolithic wall encloses the whole plot. Inside it, a U-shaped house wraps three sides of a 10 × 20 m pool, and every family room opens onto the water through glass that slides away completely.

The ground floor is a pale concrete base. The upper floor is a matte-black volume screened by white vertical fins, and it cantilevers 4 m at the master bedroom. Each of the three bars carries its own sharp gable, so one roof shape repeats three times. The palette, the fins and the repeated gable follow the direction of SoNo arhitekti's Mountain Villa in Kitzbühel.

Three gables

Each bar is a pure rectangular volume under its own 35° gable in black standing-seam metal. Gutters are concealed at the eaves, so no edge detail shows.

The void

The pool is the centre of the plan. The 7 m lobby void is the one vertical moment, and the stair floats through it.

Transparency

Frameless floor-to-ceiling glass runs 132 m along the courtyard faces. Walls pocket away and the floor continues to the pool edge at the same level.

Concealment

Nothing technical shows. Lighting is linear LED in reveals, doors and joinery are handleless, and speakers, sensors and blinds sit flush.

M-100 · Technical systems

The Systems You Never See

01 · Engineering room B.05

One basement room serves all three floors

Cooling. A water-cooled chiller makes chilled water at 6/12 °C, and dry coolers in the screened service yard reject its heat. Pumps push the water up the riser in the concrete core to fan-coil units hidden in ceiling bulkheads, one per room, each with its own thermostat. The air-handling unit supplies filtered fresh air through a heat-recovery wheel.

Water. Mains water fills two 15 m³ tanks, which hold a day of supply plus a firefighting reserve. It passes a UV steriliser, and a booster set holds 4 bar at every tap. Heat-pump cylinders on each floor provide hot water.

Power. The utility feed enters the main LV switchboard. If the grid fails, an automatic transfer switch starts the 250 kVA generator within 10 seconds. Sub-boards on each floor run off the riser, and PV in the south roof slope offsets daytime load.

02 · Server room B.11

Cooling, UPS and fire safety

The network, security recording and AV sources all live here, so the room is built to keep running when everything else fails.

CoolingIn-row precision units, N+1, holding 22 °C ± 1 and 45 % RH, independent of the house chilled water.
PowerA 10 kVA online double-conversion UPS gives about 30 minutes at full load while the generator takes over.
FireAspirating smoke detection triggers a clean-agent gas release. No water is used and the walls are 2-hour rated.
CablingOverhead ladder tray keeps copper and fibre apart. Fibre and Cat 6A run to a rack on each floor.
MonitoringLeak detection, door access and temperature alarms report to the building management system and your phone.
Adjacent storageRoom B.12 holds spares and shares the server room's controlled atmosphere.
03 · Pool plant B.10

Filtration and the hidden plant room

The water surface sits flush with the deck and spills over all four edges into a slotted stone grate. It drains to a 12 m³ balance tank under the east deck. Variable-speed pumps draw from the tank through glass-media filters, a UV steriliser, a heat pump and a salt chlorinator, and clean water returns through jets in the pool floor.

Probes track chlorine, pH and temperature and dose automatically. The plant is reached from the service corridor or a flush stainless hatch in the deck, so nothing shows in the courtyard.

04 · Smart home

One system for the whole house

Every system runs on one KNX/IP backbone with its server in B.11. Flush touch panels, a phone app and presence sensors control it, and each scene sets lighting, climate, blinds and audio together.

Frame, roof, insulation and smart glass

CantileverThe master suite projects 4 m on post-tensioned upstand beams hidden inside the upper-floor walls.
BasementWatertight concrete box inside a secant-piled perimeter. The pool has its own shell on a separate raft.
